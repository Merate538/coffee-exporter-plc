Rooted in Tradition, Exporting Excellence

GreenHarvest Coffee Export PLC, established in 2018 in Addis Ababa, is dedicated to sourcing and exporting the highest quality, ethically sourced Ethiopian green coffee beans. We bridge the gap between local farming excellence and international demand.

Our trusted export network currently spans across discerning markets in Germany, the USA, Japan, and Canada, serving importers, wholesalers, cafés, and roasting companies seeking authentic Ethiopian profiles.

Our Mission

To supply the world's finest roasters with ethically sourced, premium Ethiopian coffee while empowering local farming communities through sustainable practices.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and transparent exporter of specialty Ethiopian coffee globally, recognized for unparalleled quality and integrity.

Our Offerings

Premium Ethiopian Beans

Discover the distinct and complex flavor profiles of our meticulously processed regional coffees.

Washed & Natural

Yirgacheffe

World-renowned for its exceptional, high-altitude Arabica beans. Delivers a captivating balance of delicate flavors, often described as bright, floral, and elegant.

Origin: SNNPR Region
Altitude: 1,900 - 2,200m
Process: Washed & Natural
Flavor Notes: Tropical Fruit, Lemon, Floral, Milk Chocolate
Washed

Sidamo

An elegant coffee known for its unique sweet lemon and floral flavors. It offers an ideal balance between bright, crisp acidity and a soft, smooth body.

Origin: Sidama Region
Altitude: 1,600 - 1,900m
Process: Fully Washed
Flavor Notes: Tea-like, Lemon, Floral, Cocoa
Natural Process

Guji

Celebrated for its bright fruit and incredible sweetness. The natural drying process develops a juicy, fruit-forward character with a remarkable balance of sweetness and lively acidity.

Origin: Oromia Region
Altitude: 1,800 - 2,200m
Process: Natural Sun-Dried
Flavor Notes: Blackberry, Chocolate, Floral, Berries
Natural Process

Harrar

A dry processed, exotic bean known for its bold character and deep, expressive profile. It features a heavy body and a winey, fruity acidity that resonates in the cup.

Origin: Oromia Region (East)
Altitude: 1,400 - 2,100m
Process: Natural Sun-Dried
Flavor Notes: Blueberry, Apricot, Wine-like, Earthy
