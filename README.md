# Coffee Exporter Website

A professional website concept for a coffee export business, designed to present the company online and showcase its coffee products and export services.

live demo 
https://merate538.github.io/coffee-exporter-/

## About the Project

This project is a front-end website created as part of my web development portfolio. It explores how a coffee export company can present its business through a clear, organized, and professional online experience.

## Project Goals

- Present the coffee export business professionally.
- Showcase the company's coffee products and business offerings.
- Organize information in an easy-to-navigate website layout.
- Create a user-friendly experience for potential business customers.
- Provide a foundation for a responsive business website.
